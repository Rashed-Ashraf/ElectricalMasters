## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-reliability, industrial engineering identity tailored for electrical manufacturing, switchgear production, and critical infrastructure. The target audience comprises electrical engineers, industrial procurement directors, facility managers, and enterprise contractors who prioritize reliability, precision, and compliance over ornamental trends.

The visual style merges modern corporate utility with industrial discipline:
- **Tone:** Grounded, high-trust, technical, and durable.
- **Visual Direction:** Corporate / Modern with functional industrial accents. It employs structured white containers, high-contrast structural color blocking with deep petrol cyan and ocean teals, crisp subtle dividers, and vibrant cerulean interactive triggers.
- **Emotional Impact:** Communicates robust engineering safety, uptime confidence, and streamlined technological competence.

## Colors

The color system is organized around authentic industrial switchgear and electrical infrastructure aesthetics:

- **Primary (`#0098DA` - Electric Cerulean):** The active energetic focal color used for key interactive components, primary action buttons, active filter chips, and interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#004C6D` - Deep Petrol / Industrial Cyan):** Used for solid structural foundations, immersive dark headers, industrial contact panels, footers, and primary brand badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0A4D68` - Oceanic Dark Cyan):** Deep ambient accent for interactive depth, card headers, gradient stops, and secondary container backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#0B2545` - Crisp Navy Slate):** The anchoring dark tone for primary typography, titles, high-contrast borders, and technical data readouts.

### Functional Surfaces & Accents
- **Canvas / Tinted Background:** `#F0F8FC` (Ice Blue Tint) provides a cool, clean industrial canvas that reduces glare and enhances card separation.
- **Surface Containers:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) creates elevated, high-clarity cards and input fields.
- **Border Subtle:** `#D3E4EE` reinforces structural boundaries without visual noise.
- **Inactive / Pill Surface:** `#E1F0F8` for unselected filter pills and subtle category tags.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Montserrat** for confident, geometric headings and labels with **Roboto Flex** for readable technical prose and data attributes.

- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Rendered in bold/extrabold weights with tight tracking to evoke structural strength and manufacturing authority. Subheadings and overlines utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to act as clean industrial section markers (e.g., `OUR PRODUCTS`, `CONTACT US`).
- **Body & Specifications (Roboto Flex):** Applied at regular weights with comfortable line heights, ensuring seamless legibility across detailed technical manuals, equipment specifications, and client proposals.
- **Labels & Triggers (Montserrat):** Set in semi-bold weights for rapid recognition on buttons, navigation bars, badges, and technical filter bars.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured inside a constrained `1280px` maximum shell. It prioritizes balanced horizontal rhythm and clear demarcations between high-impact functional sections.

- **Rhythm & Scale:** An 8pt base grid is used throughout, stepping from compact `0.25rem` micro-spacers for badges up to `4.5rem` macro-spacers for major landing sections.
- **Layout Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Side-by-side industrial split panels (e.g., Contact Form + Company Metadata).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Cards reflow from 3-up to 2-up layouts.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px horizontal margins. Hero imagery, media boxes, and form panels collapse into single stacked columns with reduced heading scales.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids exaggerated blur shadows, favoring low-contrast outlines combined with shallow, crisp ambient shadows to mirror physical industrial hardware and control console enclosures:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to ice-blue page backgrounds (`#F0F8FC`) and solid petrol blue industrial blocks (`#004C6D`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white backgrounds paired with a 1px crisp outline (`border: 1px solid #D3E4EE`) and an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 76, 109, 0.05), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 76, 109, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Floating Overlays):** Used when hovering over product cards, active modals, or inspection callouts: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(0, 76, 109, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Drawers):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)` ensuring separation over contrasting canvas bands.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy follows a compact, soft approach (`roundedness: 1`). It reflects the tight-tolerance corners of engineered sheet metal enclosures, control cabinets, and modern switchgear hardware:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to input fields, regular buttons, data tables, and embedded media frames.
- **Large Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Used on product cards, form containers, modal dialogues, and prominent callout enclosures.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic interactive category filters, status chips, and certification badges to contrast against the architectural rectangularity of content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Electric Cerulean (`#0098DA`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font Montserrat SemiBold 14px, `rounded-sm` (4px). Hover: `#0084BD`. Active: `#006F9E`.
- **Secondary / Outlined Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid Electric Cerulean (`#0098DA`), text in `#0098DA`. On dark petrol sections, text and border use `#FFFFFF` or `#0098DA` with an ice-blue fill on hover.

### Filter Chips & Badges
- **Pill Chips:** Height 34px, font Montserrat SemiBold 13px, fully rounded (`rounded-full`).
  - *Active Chip:* Background `#0098DA`, text `#FFFFFF`, zero outline.
  - *Inactive Chip:* Background `#E1F0F8`, text `#004C6D`. Hover: `#D4E9F4`.
- **Status / Verification Badges:** Soft ice-blue pill badge (`#E1F0F8`) with dark cyan checkmark icons and navy-slate label text.

### Product & Service Cards
- **Architecture:** White background container (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg` (8px), structural `1px solid #D3E4EE` border.
- **Media Header:** Edge-to-edge aspect-ratio image container with top radius matching the card.
- **Content Block:** Category kicker in Electric Cerulean (`#0098DA`, 12px Montserrat Bold uppercase), product title in Navy Slate (`#0B2545`, Montserrat Bold), body description in muted slate (`#4A607A`, Roboto Flex).

### Input Fields & Forms
- **Input Elements:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border (`#C8DCE8`), `rounded-sm` (4px), padding `10px 14px`. Text in `#0B2545`, placeholder in `#8EA4B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#0098DA` with a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(0, 152, 218, 0.15)`).
- **Labels:** Montserrat SemiBold 13px in `#0B2545` with red asterisks for required fields.

### Structural Industrial Panels (Contact / Footer)
- **Deep Cyan Foundation:** Full-bleed `#004C6D` background with text in `#FFFFFF` and muted metadata in `#B0CEDB`. Form containers on this surface use pure white cards with soft elevation to create contrast against the industrial backdrop.
